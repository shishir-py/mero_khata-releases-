## Full description (max 4000)
Mero Khata (मेरो खाता) keeps track of your money without sending it anywhere.

Bank and wallet SMS become transactions you review — Global IME, Nabil, Machhapuchchhre (MBL) and many
other banks and wallets are recognised. Nothing is added until you confirm it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics
• Everything stays on your phone in an encrypted database (AES-256)
• SMS are read on the phone only; full message text is never stored
• OTPs, verification codes, promotions and personal messages are skipped
• Optional app lock with fingerprint or password

MADE FOR NEPAL
• Dates in Bikram Sambat (BS) or AD — reports by BS month and year
• NPR amounts, Nepali bank and wallet formats
• Works with Devanagari digits in messages

TRACK EVERYTHING
• Income, expenses and transfers across bank accounts, wallets and cash
• Categories, notes and search
• Monthly and yearly reports with charts
• Scheduled income and expenses (salary, rent, bills) with reminders

NEVER LOSE YOUR DATA
• Encrypted backups protected by your own password
• Automatic daily backup to a folder you choose
• Restore on a new phone after a reset

Mero Khata asks for SMS access only to find your own bank transaction messages. You can skip it and add
transactions by sharing or pasting messages, or manually.
